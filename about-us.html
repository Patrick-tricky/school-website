Our Journey

The Sacred Heart Story

Sacred Heart SSS Mushanga is a catholic founded mixed boarding secondary school located on Mushanga hill, about 3 kilometers from Kabwohe town along Mbarara-Bushenyi Highway in Sheema District. We are well known for our peaceful environment, discipline, Christian values, and commitment to academic excellence.

The School started with one campus which later divided into two due to the increased number of students. We have had a change of headteachers twice and continue to succeed under the current headteacher Mr Turyamureba Denis. We cherish the staff which has grown from a handful to now more than 200 all working in sync for the betterment of the school.
